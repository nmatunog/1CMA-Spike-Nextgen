# GIA response translation style

Owner instruction, 15 November 2026: “Always use Conversational or street Cebuano and Tagalog translations for all response translations.”

Use familiar everyday Cebuano and Tagalog, including natural English loanwords such as training, recruiter, leader, register and team. Avoid stiff, word-for-word translations or unnecessarily formal grammar. Preserve the meaning, factual scope and role boundaries of the reviewed answer.

This style instruction is not blanket approval of new answers. Keep owner-supplied wording intact, present substantive changes for wording review, and record the exact selected versions/languages. Do not introduce earnings promises, eligibility or application decisions, product advice or unsupported policy claims while making an answer sound natural. Keep program names as reviewed; a wording edit does not rename programs globally.

The style applies to response translations, not to code identifiers or behavior. It does not change consent, STOP, credentials, recruiter attribution, learning entitlements or the English-default/explicit-language-request rules.
