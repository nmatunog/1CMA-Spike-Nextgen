The first screen now has a GIA title, short AI subtitle, short greeting and three starter chips. The role selector, implementation note, duplicated notice and initial contact form are absent. Privacy, STOP, the document warning and insurance disclaimer remain visible under the composer; the single compliance paragraph wraps on mobile.

The contact form is revealed when requested or needed for booking/leader follow-up. Its fields, approved text, unticked checkbox and consent submission are unchanged. Further actions are revealed after interaction, with a separate optional “Find my path” question. Public adapter hints and `?program=intern|ra|recruit` select existing routing only; they do not verify roles or grant access.

The “What is this?” chip shows the existing GIYA/SPIKE identity line in conversation alongside existing approved Financial Literacy information. No knowledge-pack answer or credential pattern was changed. Backend safety, STOP/crisis, AIA attribution, entitlements and schema remain unchanged.

After captures are generated by `tests/gia/first-screen.spec.ts`. Before captures were taken from the exact base before editing the UI. Existing historical screenshot folders are preserved.
