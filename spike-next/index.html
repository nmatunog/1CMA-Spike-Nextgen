GIYA Institute · SPIKE / RA-SPIKE integrated program

Your Path to Financial Entrepreneurship

The integrated SPIKE Method and RA-SPIKE Rookie Academy pathway connects discovery, guided learning, professional readiness and continued development. Explore the stages without committing to a career.

01 Discover · Assessment

Begin with a preview or literacy session. Bring your goals, experience and questions.

02 Explore · Foundation

Discuss the integrated Financial Entrepreneurship Program, learning format and which entry point fits your interests.

03 Develop · Regulatory readiness

Build financial knowledge, ethics and professional skills. Discuss licensing preparation and applicable accreditation requirements; participation does not confer a license.

04 Build · Professional practice

Explore applied activities and feedback with the program team. Availability and participation requirements need confirmation.

05 Lead · Leadership development

Explore mentoring and leadership development with the team. Advancement depends on readiness, performance, regulatory requirements and available opportunities; no appointment or income is guaranteed.

06 Enterprise · Continued growth

Make an informed decision with the team about continued development and any formal application process.
